Product photos shown alongside a Work Experience card (e.g. Tesla's
Powerwall). Wired up via experience[].productImage + productCaption in
data/content.js — set both to null to hide the image for that card.

tesla-powerwall.jpg — two Powerwalls + Wall Connector, supplied directly
by the site owner. Cropped to trim excess brick wall on the left/top;
both Powerwall units are left fully intact.

range-rover-electric.jpg — Range Rover Electric marketing render,
supplied directly by the site owner. The original had "RANGE ROVER
ELECTRIC" title text and a "WAITLIST >60K" callout (with a soft glow)
composited over it; both were removed — the title by cropping the top
band, the waitlist glow/text by diffusion inpainting (fills the region
from its surrounding ground pixels, then adds matching speckle noise) so
the desert ground reads as continuous. The car and both number plates are
untouched.

log9-rapidx8000.jpg — RapidX 8000 product render, supplied directly by
the site owner, cropped tight to the product (trimmed surrounding
whitespace). An alternate "internal module" photo was also supplied but
its spec-plate text was garbled/nonsensical (fabricated units and
mixed-language text), so it wasn't used — it read as fake and would hurt
credibility on a professional portfolio. If you get a real photo of the
internal assembly, it can replace this one.

General rule for adding more of these later: don't pull a random image off
the web without checking its license. Wikimedia Commons file pages state
the license clearly; a company's own press/media page usually states its
terms of use for press photos.
